## 0. Terms
- Cohort = batch B1-B6 = one social network of regrouped SIS; sex nested (males B1, B2, B5; females B3, B4, B6); cohort structure may be biology, never nuisance; no outcome attributed to the batch.
- A1 = first active phase after a cage change, 18:30-06:30 (CC1 unless stated).
- Rate = RFID position-change rate (vendor updates >= 200 grid units per observed hour; 'position changes/hour'; identifier `crossing_rate`, never a display label); not locomotion; a gap is not stillness. Shared occupancy = shared RFID-position occupancy (`shared_zone_use`; frozen caveat, about 95% chance overlap).
- RU = 6 position changes/hour (legacy Movement_mean = rate/6 within 0.21%). Frozen SDs 5.1698 /h (s1) and 0.0607 are constants; recomputed values are gates.
- CombZ = mean of six component z-scores (NOR, sucrose_pref, weight_dev, delta_cort, adrenal_weight, spleen_weight) vs the 12 same-sex CON; higher = more resilient-like; verbatim. SIS = RES + SUS pooled, never contrasted.
- CON = one 4-animal cage per cohort, together throughout: intact pre-SIS cage in B1, B3, B4; in B2, B5, B6 four animals of a pre-SIS cage that also supplied 2 SIS.
- CEID = v2 `Batch|sys.N|CCk` (one board). Level of every estimate: cohort, cage_within_cohort, source_cage_within_cohort, animal_within_cohort, within_animal, descriptive_record.
- Paths: `<ROOT>` `mmm_project_root()`; `<AR>` `<ROOT>/analysis_ready`; `<EP>` `mmm_endpoint_source_root()`; `<PL>` `dirname(<EP>)/Planning`; `<SL>` `dirname(<ROOT>)/SLEAPanalyzer_v2/releases/2026-09-22_exp9_all-batches_canonical-complete-figures-v3`; `<A4>` `MMM_S33_A4_DIR`; no data-root literal in code.

## 1. Questions
- A (cohort level): which components carry the covariance of the CC1 A1 rate with CombZ across the six SIS cohort means, and how much of each SIS cohort offset is shared with the cohort's CON cage? Secondary: within-cohort component slopes (individual level; unadjusted and with CC1-day weight tp2); raw corticosterone; per-episode weight gains vs that episode's A1 rate; descriptors. Across cohorts weight and age travel with the rate (r 0.98, 0.62).
- B: (a) within-cohort SIS associations of source cage, age with tp1 (jointly) and substrain with the rate and CombZ; variance split. (b) B2 and B6 (selected for the lowest SIS A1 rate of their sex): ranks among the three same-sex cohorts in SLEAP arena distance about three weeks later; same-board table. No SIS-vs-CON rate difference.
- C: split of the frozen Stage 29 v1.0.1 within-cohort SIS slope of CombZ on x (rate, shared occupancy): beta_W within the CC1 cage; beta_B between CC1 cages within cohort (aggregate of own-animal and cage-mate parts; = beta_W without cage-mate association); gamma = beta_B - beta_W, the contextual (peer-composition) term (with S3's delta_peer the only cage-mate part). Sex-averaged; re-slices seen numbers.
- D (cohort level): hourly separation of SIS cohort means in CC1 A1 (clock hour; hours since recording start), CON separately; recurrence of the SIS ordering at CC2-CC4. No CombZ, no SIS-minus-CON.
- E: within cohort, adjusted for own CC1 A1 rate, does CombZ covary more with the CC1 A1 rate of CC1 cage-mates than of other classes (never co-housed; later cage-mates; never co-housed from another recorded pre-SIS cage)? Only class contrasts identified; FE companions by pre-SIS cage; secondary versions with later rates and CC1-day weight. E's m1 = C's leave-one-out cage-mate mean / s1 exactly, so E's CC1-class contrasts and C's contextual term are one association.
- Seen before the freeze (1.6): Stage 29/29b/30/32 results (null H01, H03, H11; secondary H13); the 2026-10-01 diagnostic; planning values (A weight relations, tp2-adjusted slopes, SE ratios, re-pairing ranges, E5 rows; B E3-E5 fits and arena dry run with B2, B6 SIS highest of their sex in arena distance; C Stage 29 rows and placebo designs; D B3 prototypes; E scratch prints, 12); review 2026-10-05: within-animal lag-1 correlations of weight gains (weights only; SIS -0.33, -0.14, +0.03; CON -0.30, -0.24, -0.59) and the arena ranks. Choices were made with these in view; none aimed at a value; every planned row is reported.

## 17. Wording rules
### 17.1 Stage-wide (README: category form)
1. 'post hoc, descriptive (estimation only)' on every table (`tier`), README and sentence; method and basis named; no p-values, tests or test words.
2. 'Cohort' = one batch = one social network; may be biology, never nuisance; no outcome attributed to the batch; 'six cohorts cannot separate cohort biology from cohort-level procedure, assay runs or CC1 body size'.
3. Cohort-level and individual-level statements in separate sentences naming their level; no transfer; no individual predictive claim.
4. 'RFID position-change rate (position changes per observed hour) in the first active phase after CC1 (A1, 18:30-06:30)'; never locomotion, activity, movement or distance; 'arena distance' = SLEAP cm; 'shared RFID-position occupancy' with its caveat; Movement_mean only as legacy identifier.
